# Media provenance: Halloween Horror Nights and Knott's Scary Farm

Audit date: 2026-09-28. The full, machine-readable list is
[`media-manifest.json`](media-manifest.json), regenerated by
`npx tsx scripts/media-manifest.ts`.

**No artwork was generated.** Every image recorded here exists on its owner's
site, or as a copy that is labelled as such. A record with no artwork keeps the
designed fallback card, which is the app's intended presentation for it.

## Policy

Haunt Ranker is offline first. **The app never loads an image from the web.**
It shows only images it holds: offline copies in its own media folder, files a
user added, and assets approved to ship (there are none). Everything else is
listed on the wiki page with its provenance and a **View at source** link.

| Classification          | Distribution | What happens                                                      |
| ----------------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| approved asset          | `bundled`    | may ship with the app — none are approved                         |
| user-provided           | `local`      | a copy in the app's media folder, shown, never shipped            |
| external reference only | `reference`  | the owner's original — linked; an offline copy may be kept        |
| redistribution unclear  | `unclear`    | a copy elsewhere, rights unestablished — linked; copy may be kept |

The dataset and pack record `reference` or `unclear`, so they describe the
archive truthfully to anyone they're shared with. `npm run media:download`
turns a record into a personal offline copy on one machine (`local`) and keeps
its original URL, credit and licence note. The copies are never committed and
never bundled.

**The permission this rests on is personal use.** Both owners' terms allow
personal, non-commercial use, and the copies are made for exactly that. Neither
allows redistribution, so none of this artwork may be bundled into a build
that's given to other people.

### Licensing, as stated by the owners

- **Universal Media Site:** download is "for news reporting and editorial
  purposes only", and the content "may not be used for commercial purposes".
- **Universal Orlando and Universal Studios Hollywood terms of use:** a
  "limited revocable licence to use the Site or service for your own personal
  and non-commercial use". Nothing may be reproduced or displayed "unless
  expressly permitted by Universal in writing".
- **Discover Universal:** "© Universal Studios. All rights reserved." It states
  no reuse licence, so Universal's terms apply.
- **Wayback Machine copies:** the rights remain Universal's. The archive's copy
  grants none.
- **Six Flags Terms of Use (Knott's):** all rights reserved. The only exception
  is limited, temporary display "for personal, educational, noncommercial
  purposes". The Knott's Media Center (via Dropbox) is for credentialed media,
  and Knott's PR (pr.clerk@sixflags.com) is the route to any wider permission.
- **Licensed houses** (Stranger Things, Fallout, Hellraiser, Ghostbusters, A
  Quiet Place, Terrifier, WWE and others) also carry the IP holder's rights.
  Each record's licence note names the franchise.

## Offline copies on this machine

- **132 copies, 19.8 MB in total**, in the app's own media folder.
- 117 of them are the host's own resized rendition (about 1,024 pixels wide),
  not the original: WordPress's stored sizes, or Sanity's on-request resize.
  The full-resolution originals would have come to about 99 MB.
- `npm run media:download -- --check` reports 0 problems.
- Every stored path is `media/<safe generated name>`, relative to the app's
  data folder. None refers to where the file came from, and every row still
  records its original URL.
- Re-importing the HHN dataset or the Knott's pack updates a record's URL,
  credit and licence note and keeps the copy. Both importers have tests for
  this.
- **Kept as records only, no copy:**
  - The four maps: PDFs and map images aren't downloaded, and a map is never
    card art.
  - The Theme Park Duo copies of The Grimoire (18 MB) and Mesmer (48 MB). Both
    are over the 8 MB cap, and that site offers no smaller rendition.

## Unresolved artwork

Missing artwork is acceptable. These records show the fallback card.

**Knott's Scary Farm (11)**

- Season key art for **2024** and **2025**. Only maps were found; the 2026 key
  art is recorded.
- Mazes and zones: **Wax Works**, **Bloodline 1842** and **The Gauntlet
  (2024)**. The 2026 Gauntlet art is on the reimagined record.
- Mazes recorded but with no copy kept (see above): **The Grimoire** and
  **Mesmer: Sideshow of the Mind**. No official copy exists.
- Special experiences: Timber Mountain Log Ride: Halloween Hootenanny, Calico
  Candy Mine, Yours Cruelly, Elvira EXXperience, Into the Fog, Legacy Haunt
  Store & Museum, and Scaremony.

**Halloween Horror Nights (303 houses and zones)**

- Every season 2010–2026 has at least one recorded image.
- Official per-house art was researched only for 2024–2026; 14 records in those
  years have none:
  - 2024: The Texas Chainsaw Massacre: The Legacy of Leatherface; zones
    Luchadores Monstruosos, Murder of Crowz, Chainsaw Punkz, Skull Lordz.
  - 2025: Monstruos 3: The Ghosts of Latin America; zones Mel's Die-In Zombies,
    Club Horror, Carnival of Carnage, Noche de Brujas, Chainsaw Clownz, Murder
    of Crowz.
  - 2026: zones Mel's Die-In Zombies, Club Horror.
- 2010–2023: individual houses and zones weren't researched, apart from Face
  Off (2014), Stranger Things (2018, 2019), Ghostbusters (2019) and the 2021
  Terror Tram. For those years, 289 records show the fallback.
- Season-level gaps: no event logo or key art was found for Hollywood in 2010,
  2018, 2019, 2021 or 2023. For 2018, archived Hollywood captures exist but
  didn't respond when checked.
- Official event maps: only Orlando 2014, via the Wayback Machine. Maps for
  recent years appear to be published only in the apps. The Orlando 2024 map
  PDF no longer resolves.

## For review

- **Face Off: In the Flesh (2014).** The archive files the Orlando record as a
  scare zone, but Universal's key art bills it as a house. The art is attached
  to the Orlando record as found; the record's type may need correcting.
- **Grimoire and Mesmer.** The only imagery for them is a 48 MB and an 18 MB
  third-party copy. A copy could be kept by raising the cap
  (`--max-mb 50`), or the records can be left without one.
- **The SmugMug "Official-Images" gallery** serves the 2025 map, but its
  operator couldn't be confirmed, so the map is marked `unclear`.
- **Distributing the app.** The offline copies live only in each
  installation's data folder, so a build handed to someone else carries none
  of them. Each person would run `npm run media:download` for their own
  personal use.
