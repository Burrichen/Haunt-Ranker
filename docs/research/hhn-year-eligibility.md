# HHN year eligibility research

**Status: for human review. Nothing here has been entered into the app.**

This decides _which event years the Haunt Ranker archive should cover_, on one
test: **is there public YouTube material good enough to support an archive
entry?** It does not contain attraction descriptions, and no application code
was changed to produce it.

- Researched: **19 September 2026**.
- Scope: Universal Orlando and Universal Studios Hollywood, every year either
  ran the event.
- Result: **35 of 35 Orlando event years recommended for inclusion**, and
  **20 of 26 Hollywood event years**, with Hollywood 1986–2000 recommended for
  exclusion. Details and reasoning below.

> **The archive starts at 2010.** After this research was reviewed, the project
> owner set a hard cap: **2010 is the earliest year Haunt Ranker covers**, for
> both parks. That is a scope decision, not a correction — the eligibility
> findings below stand, and the years between 1991 and 2009 remain eligible on
> the evidence if the cap is ever lifted. Everything downstream, starting with
> [the attraction catalogue](hhn-attraction-catalogue.md), is built from 2010
> onwards only. Rows below for earlier years are kept because they record what
> is available, not because they are queued for entry.

## How this was researched

1. **Year lists** came from Wikipedia and the Halloween Horror Nights Wiki, and
   were cross-checked against the event names visible in verified video titles.
2. **Availability** was tested by querying YouTube search directly for each
   park-year, using several phrasings per year where the first result set was
   thin. Searching for "HHN 2008 Hollywood maze" and searching for "Halloween
   Horror Nights 2008 Hollywood footage" do not return the same thing — two
   years in this table changed tier on a reworded second attempt, so a single
   negative search was never treated as an answer.
3. **Every YouTube URL cited below was verified** through YouTube's oEmbed
   endpoint on the research date, which returns the real title and channel and
   fails for anything private, deleted or non-existent. All 148 URLs cited here
   passed; one further candidate did not respond and was dropped rather than
   cited. Titles below are as returned by YouTube, not as remembered or
   paraphrased.
4. **No URL appears here that wasn't returned by a search and then verified.**

### What the tiers mean

| Tier  | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A** | Multiple contemporaneous videos including walkthroughs, POV or long-form event coverage. Enough to describe individual attractions.                                                      |
| **B** | Contemporaneous footage exists but is partial — short clips, commercials, parade or behind-the-scenes only. Enough to confirm what ran and its atmosphere; thin for room-by-room detail. |
| **C** | No contemporaneous footage found. Only later retrospectives, histories or scanned programmes.                                                                                            |
| **D** | Nothing usable found.                                                                                                                                                                    |

A and B are recommended for inclusion. C is recommended for exclusion _for now_
— the year is real and documented in text, but the archive would have nothing
to show. D is excluded.

## Notes on the uncertain and excluded cases

**Note 1 — Hollywood 1986 is a different thing, and a grim one.** The 1986
event was a haunted tram tour rather than the maze-based event this archive
models, and it was discontinued after a fatal accident. The only material found
is a modern retrospective about that accident ([The Horrific Universal Studios
Terror Tram Tragedy on Halloween in 1986!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sa6QSA4J0Q),
Paul Le). There is no event footage, and the format doesn't match. Recommend
excluding it from the archive and, if it is ever mentioned, treating it as
historical context on the Hollywood 1992 or 2006 entry rather than as a season
with attractions.

**Note 2 — Orlando 1993 and 1994 are the thinnest "include" calls.** Both have
contemporaneous footage, but only short compilations (2–3 minutes) rather than
anything attraction-by-attraction. They are recommended for inclusion because
the footage exists, the years are well documented in text, and excluding two
years from the middle of an otherwise unbroken Orlando run would leave a hole a
reader would notice. Expect their wiki pages to stay short.

**Note 3 — Hollywood 2006–2010 is Terror Tram-heavy.** Most surviving footage
from Hollywood's return years covers the Terror Tram and general event
atmosphere rather than individual mazes. The years are includable; the maze
entries will lean on text sources more than on video.

**Note 4 — 2026 is in progress.** The current season runs 28 August – 1
November 2026 at Orlando, and material is still being uploaded. Anything
recorded for 2026 should be treated as provisional until the season ends.

**Hollywood 1992 and 1997–2000: excluded, but not empty.** These years are
real, and basic facts exist — the HHN Wiki records, for example, four mazes in
1997 and five in 1999 under the slogan "Last Gasp". What does not exist, across
several differently-worded searches, is any contemporaneous video. The only
Hollywood material found for that whole era is [a history of the early
years](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2EgEd_08WuE) (Haunt Season) and [scanned
event programmes 1992–2019](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dh56NYxEpYE) (Keena
Kindragon). **This is the decision most worth a second opinion**: if you would
rather the archive list these seasons with sourced facts and no footage, they
can be included as tier C, and the table's recommendation reversed. The
research says only that they fail the YouTube test.

**One citation has an unresolved park.** [Universals HHN - 1999](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gm67kCP0yLs)
(P Stutts, 18:35) is substantial 1999 footage, but the title doesn't say which
park and it surfaced under searches for both. It is cited for Orlando 1999
below on the strength of the other Orlando-specific material around it —
**confirm the park before relying on it.**

**Uploader titles are not always right.** Two 1998 Orlando house videos are
titled "Halloween Horror Nights XIII" (which would be 2003) while describing
1998 attractions. Treat the year in a title as a claim to check, not a fact.

## Questions for you before data entry

These came out of the research and are decisions about the app, not about the
data. **No code has been changed.**

1. ~~**Hollywood's Terror Tram doesn't fit `house` or `scare_zone`.**~~
   **Decided: it is recorded as a scare zone**, on your call, and no schema
   change is needed. It is an open-air walk through a themed backlot area with
   roaming scareactors, which is what a scare zone is; filing it under houses
   would have put a tram-and-walk experience into rankings beside enclosed maze
   builds. See [the attraction catalogue](hhn-attraction-catalogue.md), which
   carries 19 of them.
2. **Orlando's venue moved.** HHN ran at Islands of Adventure in 2002, 2003 and
   2005, and across both Orlando parks in 2004. The archive's park axis is
   Orlando vs Hollywood, which is still correct for provenance — the specific
   park can go in an attraction's `location`. Worth confirming you're happy
   with that before entry, because it can't be retrofitted cheaply.
3. **Shows are out of scope as things stand.** Bill & Ted's Excellent Halloween
   Adventure, the Festival of the Dead parade and Frightmares are heavily
   represented in the archival footage — several of the best 1990s videos are
   _of the shows_ — but the schema has no type for them.

## Factual sources (not YouTube)

YouTube decides eligibility; these are what facts should be checked against
during entry.

- **Official** — [Universal Orlando HHN](https://www.universalorlando.com/web/en/us/things-to-do/events/halloween-horror-nights) · [Universal Studios Hollywood HHN](https://www.universalstudioshollywood.com/web/en/us/things-to-do/events/halloween-horror-nights) · [Universal Destinations press site](https://media.universalparksusa.com/press-releases/halloween-horror-nights-2026-overview/) (the 2026 overview release confirms the season's dates and house count)
- **Encyclopaedic** — [Wikipedia: Halloween Horror Nights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halloween_Horror_Nights) (used for the year lists, the Hollywood hiatus and the 2020 cancellation)
- **Fan archives** — [Halloween Horror Nights Wiki](https://halloweenhorrornights.fandom.com/) (per-year and per-attraction pages for both parks; its Hollywood 1997–2000 pages are sparse and carry editor requests for help) · [HHN Legacy](https://www.hhnlegacy.com/) (Orlando 1991–2026, including house location history)
- **Press/guides** — [Orlando Informer's HHN guide](https://orlandoinformer.com/universal/halloween-horror-nights/) · WDWNT's "30 Years of Fear" per-year history series (indexed in search; the site blocks automated fetches, so check it in a browser)

Fan archives are secondary sources. Where a fact matters — a name, a year, a
park — it should be traceable to Universal's own material or to contemporaneous
press, with the fan archive as corroboration.
