## Revisions after review

- **The Gauntlet** is two records: the 2024 zone, and the version Knott's billed as reimagined, which ran in 2025 and 2026. The second is linked to the first as a reimagining.

## Merge and version decisions

- **A returning maze or zone is one record.** It is filed under its first season in this pack, and its later seasons are recorded as appearances, so its wiki page reads "Appeared in: 2024, 2025, 2026". No returning maze was materially re-themed according to any source.
- **The Gauntlet** is split, as agreed in review.
- **CarnEVIL 2026** is officially "reimagined", but it stays one record, as agreed in review. The change is written up, with a link to Mesmer.
- **Debut years** are set only where Knott's billed the record as new, or where two independent sources agree. The pre-2024 debuts rest on Wikipedia plus press guides, not on Knott's.
- **Final-year status** is written into each record's development notes, with sources. The 2026 finales for The Chilling Chambers and Origins come from six press reports of Knott's announcement; the official 2026 page doesn't mark them.

## Uncertain records

- **Calico Candy Mine** rests on two sources, both MouseInfo. Whether it ran at Scary Farm in 2025 or 2026 is unresolved, so it appears in 2024 only.
- **Scaremony** rests on two sources and is documented for 2026 only, though it probably runs every year.
- **Maze building locations:** building-level detail such as "beside GhostRider" and "Wilderness Dance Hall" comes from fan wikis, matched against the markers on the official 2025 map. Bloodline 1842 has no confirmed location and none is given.
- **Minor conflicts:**
  - Eight Fingers Nine is set in the 18th century in official copy and the 17th in the designer feature.
  - The Keeper is announced for The Chilling Chambers in 2024 but reported absent.
  - Inked is placed on the Boardwalk by two sources and in Ghost Town by one.
  - Night counts vary: 2025 was announced as 28 nights, with a report that two Wednesdays were dropped; 2026 is 26 nights in most sources and 30 in one.

## Missing information

- Knott's own 2024 and 2025 event pages are gone: knotts.com now redirects to Six Flags, which shows 2026, and the Wayback Machine couldn't be reached. Those seasons are sourced from press-release reprints and the official 2025 map.
- Scare zone debut years are left unset because only Wikipedia gives them. As a result the 2024 season page will list the zones as not yet placed rather than as "returning".
- There is no official per-maze key art at a Knott's-owned URL. The only media are two 2025 Knott's press images, rehosted by Theme Park Duo, and the official 2025 map. All are marked `reference`, never `bundled`.
- Named characters are sparse for The Zoo and Unearthed. Inked's cartoon characters (Clancy Cat, Ginny Ghost, Petey Pumpkin) appear in only one review and are left out.
- No 2025- or 2026-specific video was found for the Hootenanny or the scare zones. Several returning 2026 mazes are covered only by all-maze compilations.
- Left out as unconfirmed or not attractions: Coasters in the Dark, the 2026 Blind Tiger Balcony Soirée, lanterns and Color Collect, Boo-fet dining, and Meet the Creep.

## Source coverage

99 sources: 1 official site, 5 Knott's press releases and official map postings, 39 articles, 53 YouTube videos and 1 Wikipedia page. Every source is cited by at least one record or season.

- Every maze cites 8–19 sources, including at least one Knott's source and at least two walkthrough videos.
- Every scare zone record cites 6–13 sources.
- Special experiences cite 2–9 sources.

Reviews were used only for observed facts, such as scenes, effects and changes, and never for lore.

## App behaviour found while preparing this, and fixed during the import

- **Years overview:** it counted only records filed under a season, so 2025 and 2026 showed 2 mazes and 0 zones. It now counts everything that appeared in the season, as season pages already did.
- **Year statistics and the Statistics page** counted special experiences as unreviewed, for example "0 of 18 reviewed". They now count only walk-throughs and scare zones, which are the only categories that take a review.
- **Wiki pages** offered a Theme / Fun / Fear review for any type, and cards showed "Not Rated" for everything. Special experiences now get neither.
- **An overlay's back link** went to Scare Zones. It now returns to the record's season.
